# LinkedIn Rules

- Length: 150–300 words is the sweet spot. Hard maximum 3,000 characters.
- Tone: professional but personal. First-person stories outperform corporate
  announcements.
- The first 2–3 lines show before the "...see more" fold. The hook must land
  before the fold.
- Line breaks are your friend: single-sentence lines read better than dense
  paragraphs on mobile.
- Tag people or companies only when they are genuinely part of the story.
- Emojis: sparing use is fine (2–4 per post), as bullets or section markers,
  never mid-sentence strings.
- Links in the post body suppress reach; put links in the first comment and say
  "link in comments".
- Best posting windows: Tue–Thu, 8–10am local time.
