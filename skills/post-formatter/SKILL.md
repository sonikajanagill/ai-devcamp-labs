# Post Formatter

Apply these structural rules to every post draft:

1. **Hook first line.** The first line must stop the scroll: a bold claim, a
   surprising number, or a direct question. Never open with "I'm excited to
   announce" or similar throat-clearing.
2. **Maximum 3 short paragraphs.** Each paragraph 1–3 sentences. White space
   between paragraphs.
3. **Maximum 5 hashtags**, placed at the end of the post, never inline.
4. **No em dashes.** Use commas, full stops, or parentheses instead.
5. End with either a question to the reader or a clear call to action, not both.
